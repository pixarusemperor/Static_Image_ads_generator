The goal is to **not look like an ad.** People have "Ad Blindness," but they have a biological addiction to news and gossip.

*   **Raw Native:** An iPhone-quality photo (imperfect lighting) of a person. This builds trust.
*   **Breaking News:** A high-contrast image with a black bottom bar and white/yellow "Breaking News" text.
*   **The "Secret Info":** A wide-angle landscape shot (e.g., a suburb or a factory) with a small map overlay.
*   **SMS/Notification:** A screenshot of a text message or a bank alert overlaying a native photo.

### 2. The Visual Anchor (The "Sauce")
Every image must contain these three elements to force the brain to engage:
*   **The Primary Subject:** A person looking intense, shocked, or focused.
*   **The Intrigue Circle:** A small circular crop overlaying the image showing a "clue" (e.g., a dashboard, a specific object, a "hack").
*   **The Red Callout:** A hand-drawn style red arrow or circle connecting the main subject to the "clue." 
    *   *Why?* It forces the brain to try and solve the mystery of the relationship between the two objects.

---

## PHASE 3: COPYWRITING FRAMEWORKS
We use the **"Greasiness Scale"**—every sentence must make the next sentence impossible not to read.

### 1. The Eyeball-Grabbing Headline
*   **Formula:** [Target Audience] + [Action Word] + [Intrigue Trigger] + [Specific Result].
*   *Example:* "Aussie Homeowners Going Wild After Spotting Mortgage Hack (Saves $856/mo)."
*   *Rule:* Use news-style fonts. Avoid "Corporate Speak."

### 2. The Slippery Lead-In (First 3 Lines)
*   **Objective:** Open a loop that can only be closed by clicking "See More."
*   *Rule:* Use short, punchy sentences. 
*   *Logic:* "This client-getting hack feels illegal..." or "Former Google worker issues alarming AI warning..."

*   **Readability:** Aim for **Grade 4 or 5** on the Hemingway App.
*   **Formatting:** One sentence per line. Lots of white space.
*   **Vocabulary:** Use one and two-syllable words. No "marketing jargon."
*   **The Pivot:** Transition from the "News Hook" into your "Specific Big Benefit" smoothly.

---

## PHASE 4: COMPLIANCE & THE FINAL BUILD
To ensure the ad isn't flagged by the algorithm while maintaining a high Click-Through Rate (CTR):

1.  **Avoid the word "YOU":** Ad platforms hate direct call-outs. 
    *   *Bad:* "You need this tool to grow your business."

2.  **The Non-Threatening CTA:** Always use **"Learn More."**

3.  **The Specificity Rule:** Never say "Save money." Say "Save $1,422.50." Generalizations cause doubt; specificity creates belief.
