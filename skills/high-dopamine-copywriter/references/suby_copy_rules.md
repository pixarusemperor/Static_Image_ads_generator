# Sabri Suby's Core Copywriting Laws

Synthesized from over **$200 million in Meta ad spend** and generating **$7.8 billion in tracked client sales**, these principles form the bedrock of Sabri Suby's high-dopamine direct-response methodology.

---

## 1. The 3% vs. 97% Market Law

In any market at any given moment:
- **3% are ready to buy right now:** Every competitor is bidding furiously on keyword search and bottom-funnel retargeting for this tiny segment.
- **17% are in information gathering mode:** They know they want something, but are comparing vendors.
- **20% are problem aware:** They feel the acute symptom, but don't know a solution exists.
- **60% are UNAWARE:** They do not even know they have a problem.

**The Golden Rule:** The advertiser who can articulate the problem best to the **60% unaware group** and guide them through education becomes the undisputed market leader. Broad targeting with high-curiosity news hooks is the only weapon that penetrates this layer.

---

## 2. The Greasy Chute Principle

> *"The sole purpose of the first sentence is to get them to read the second sentence. The sole purpose of the second sentence is to get them to read the third sentence."*

- An ad should never try to sell the entire product in the opening lines.
- It sells **micro-commitments of attention**.
- If a sentence feels heavy, clunky, or self-important, the reader slips off the chute.

---

## 3. The Curiosity Gap & Information Asymmetry

- Direct-response legend George Loewenstein proved that curiosity occurs when there is a gap between what we know and what we want to know.
- **Never reveal the vehicle in the hook.**
- If your product is an automated accounting tool, do not say: *"Try our new automated accounting software."*
- Say: *"The $14 black drive that audited a 40-person agency in 8 minutes."*
- The mind is biologically compelled to close the loop by clicking.

---

## 4. Specific Odd Numbers Over Round Estimates

- Humans are conditioned to recognize marketing round numbers ($1,000, 50%, 100 leads) as fictitious marketing hype.
- Exact odd numbers imply that an actual measurement took place:
  - `$1,422.50` vs. `$1,500`
  - `312 agencies` vs. `300 agencies`
  - `14.8 minutes` vs. `15 minutes`
  - `90 to 480 accounts` vs. `500 accounts`

---

## 5. The "Learn More" Gateway vs. The "Buy Now" Defense Barrier

- Clicking `"Buy Now"` implies an immediate financial commitment, triggering anxiety and cognitive evaluation.
- Clicking `"Learn More"` implies zero obligation; it satisfies curiosity.
- The objective of the ad is **never to close the sale on the platform**; the objective is to earn the qualified click to the pre-sell landing page.

---

## 6. Social Proof Stacking

Cold traffic does not trust you. They trust peer consensus:
- Use specific ratings: `"4.9 ⭐ (312 verified agency audits)"`.
- Quoted conversational snippets: *"'We found $9,400 in our first 48 hours' — Operations Lead, Austin TX"*.
- Authentic engagement cues: Reposts, unedited screenshots, verified author badges.
