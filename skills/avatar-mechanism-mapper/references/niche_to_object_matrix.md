## Photography & Visual Composition Guardrails

1. **Anti-Stock Imperfection:**
   - Never use polished corporate models or pristine stock photo studio backgrounds.
   - Inject realistic clutter: coffee cups, pens, wrinkled paper, natural office lighting, authentic ISO camera grain.

2. **Negative Space Allocation:**
   - The Mystery Object must be situated in the **upper-left** or **upper-right** quadrant (top 15%–45% of vertical space).
   - This leaves the lower 35%–40% clear for gradient overlays, tabloid breaking news bars, and high-contrast headlines.

3. **Focal Clarity for Magnified Insets:**
   - The object must be visually distinct enough that a 200x200px circular cutout remains instantly legible when overlaid with a 4px colored border.
