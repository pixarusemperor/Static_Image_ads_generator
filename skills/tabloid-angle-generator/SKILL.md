# Tabloid Angle Generator

The `tabloid-angle-generator` produces scroll-stopping news, leak, and curiosity angles engineered specifically for cold traffic on Meta platforms.

Modeled on Facebook's quarterly **Most Viewed Content Reports**—where publisher giants like **LADbible (126.4M views)**, **Unilad (117M views)**, **E! News (116M views)**, and **TMZ (89M views)** consistently outperform traditional commercial advertisers—this skill wraps commercial offers into viral journalistic narratives.

Crucially, it enforces Sabri Suby's **Honey vs. Vinegar Rule**: positive benefit-driven curiosity outpulls negative fear-mongering 8 out of 10 times in cold newsfeeds.

## The Newsfeed Attention Science

Facebook publishes the *Widely Viewed Content Report* every quarter. The data consistently demonstrates:
1. **Users do not open Facebook or Instagram to buy software, supplements, or consulting.**
2. **Users open social media for gossip, shocking discoveries, human drama, and news.**
3. **Ads formatted as commercial announcements suffer massive banner blindness.**
4. **Ads formatted as breaking news or investigative leaks command instant engagement.**

## The "Honey vs. Vinegar" Polarity Constraint

### The Rookie Mistake (Vinegar):
Inexperienced copywriters instinctively lean into negative fear, shame, or despair:
- ❌ *"Don't let your agency go bankrupt in 2026."*
- ❌ *"Are you tired of failing on your fitness journey?"*
- ❌ *"The terrifying truth about why your business is dying."*

**Result:** Triggers defensive ad filtering, depressing emotional associations, and Meta policy flags for negative self-perception.

### The Sabri Suby Law (Honey):
Sabri Suby's empirical ad spend ($200M+) proves that **positive benefit hooks promise outsized rewards and outpull negative avoidance hooks 8 out of 10 times**:
- ✅ *"How a 24-year-old added 90–480 inbound clients in 8 weeks using an unlisted database."*
- ✅ *"The bizarre $14 morning ritual that helped 1,420 busy professionals drop 12 to 19 kgs."*
- ✅ *"Why 312 marketing agencies unlocked an extra $12,400/month without hiring new staff."*

Every angle generated by this skill must balance intrigue with an irresistible, positive aspirational transformation.

## Workflows

### 1. Archetype Instantiation
Take the offer mechanism and synthesize 5 tailored variants:

1. **Whistleblower / Leak:**
   - *Formula:* `[Industry Role] breaks silence on [Proprietary Protocol] that generates [Specific Gain]`
   - *Example:* "Former Google ads manager reveals the unlisted broad-targeting protocol generating $14,200/day."

2. **Accidental Discovery:**
   - *Formula:* `[Specific Persona] stumbled upon [Bizarre Habit/Tool] while trying to [Solve Minor Problem]`
   - *Example:* "Agency owner spotted a 4-line Slack setting that accidentally uncovered $12,400 in lost billable hours."

3. **Authority Counter-Punch:**
   - *Formula:* `Why [Established Authority/Gurus] are furious over this $0 [Mechanism Name]`
   - *Example:* "Why Wall Street fund managers are questioning this 2026 mortgage calculation protocol."

4. **Secret Geography / Asset:**
   - *Formula:* `The unlisted [Database/Hardware/Method] that [Produced Massive Result] in [Exact Days]`
   - *Example:* "The unindexed 14-page directory that delivered 312 qualified clients without ad spend."

5. **The Warning / New Era:**
   - *Formula:* `Why [Institutions/Professionals] are quietly transitioning to [Mechanism Name] before [Year]`
   - *Example:* "Why 40-person agencies are quietly swapping timesheet software for background packet monitors."

### 2. Polarity Calibration Check
Calculate the **Honey Polarity Score (0–100)**:
- Must score $\ge 75$.
- Deduct points for: fear-mongering words (*dying, disaster, bankruptcy, humiliation*), direct personal accusations (*you are failing*), or negative clinical jargon.
- Add points for: specific odd metrics ($12,400, 90–480), curiosity gaps, positive empowerment, and institutional validation.
