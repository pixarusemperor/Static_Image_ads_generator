# Viral News Frameworks & Headline Science

To consistently generate static image ads that achieve disproportionate engagement and low customer acquisition costs, creative strategy must study what actually commands attention on social media.

---

## 1. The Facebook Transparency Center Empirical Benchmark

Every quarter, Meta publishes its **Widely Viewed Content Report** (WVCR) summarizing the most viewed posts, pages, and domains across Facebook. 

The top-ranking media properties by quarterly impressions consistently include:
- **LADbible:** 126.4M+ quarterly views
- **Unilad:** 117.0M+ quarterly views
- **E! News:** 116.2M+ quarterly views
- **People Magazine:** 109.1M+ quarterly views
- **TMZ:** 89.4M+ quarterly views
- **LADbible Australia:** 86.8M+ quarterly views

### Key Structural Findings
1. **Third-Person Reporting Voice:** Headlines never speak directly to the viewer in a pushy second-person sales tone. They report on an external event, person, discovery, or controversy.
2. **The Observation Gap:** The user is placed in the position of an observer looking at something unusual or unreleased.
3. **High-Dopamine Chromatic Split:** Black background with high-contrast White text paired with vibrant Tabloid Yellow (`#FFE500`) or News Alert Red (`#E50914`).
4. **Concrete Realism:** Real candid photos, imperfect phone captures, or dramatic candid moments beat glossy commercial artwork every single time.

## 2. The 12 Viral News Triggers

| # | Trigger Name | Underlying Human Curiosity Mechanism | Exemplar Headline Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **The Insider Leak** | Desire for forbidden or confidential access | `DATA LEAK: Industry standard [X] replaced by [Proprietary Object]` |
| 2 | **The Accidental Fluke** | Hope that massive gains can occur with minimal effort | `[Role] stumbled onto [Result] after a routine [Everyday Action]` |
| 3 | **The Obsolete Standard** | Fear of being left behind on outdated practices | `Why top [Group] are quietly ditching [Standard Tool] in [Year]` |
| 4 | **The Institutional Feud** | Thrill of witnessing an underdog defeat a monopoly | `Why [Giant Monopolies] are lobbying against this $0 [Object]` |
| 5 | **The Hidden Geography** | Exclusive access to unlisted assets | `The unindexed [Directory/Tool] delivering [Odd Result] in [Days]` |
| 6 | **The Silent Shift** | Social proof that a major change is already underway | `Why [Exact Number] of [Professionals] switched to [New Method]` |
| 7 | **The Redacted Report** | Suspicion that official institutions withhold breakthroughs | `REVEALED: The unreleased 2026 data study on [Transformation]` |
| 8 | **The Bizarre Ritual** | Curiosity about unusual, counter-intuitive actions | `The 1 [Habit/Object] [Recognized Group] do before [Event]` |
| 9 | **The Specific Outlier** | Fascination with non-standard numerical outcomes | `How [Name/Role] unlocked [Odd Metric] without [Expected Pain]` |
| 10 | **The Flaw Exposed** | Relief that past failures were caused by an external bug | `EXPOSED: The hidden [Fee/Glitch] costing [Group] [Odd Dollar Amount]` |
| 11 | **The Banned Loophole** | Perception of an unfair, high-margin competitive advantage | `Why industry whistleblowers are questioning this [Metric] protocol` |
| 12 | **The Reverse Warning** | A warning that actually reveals an incredible opportunity | `WARNING: Do not implement [Old Method] until reviewing this [Object]` |

## 3. Headline Punctuation, Capitalization & Syntax Rules

1. **Lead Keyword Highlighting:** Use a capitalized news ticker label followed by a colon:
   - `REVEALED:`
   - `DATA LEAK:`
   - `ALERT:`
   - `EXPOSED:`
   - `NOTICE:`
2. **Square Bracket Keyword Syntax (for Template 2-a):**
   - In Template `2-a`, enclose the central curiosity keyword in brackets to render a vibrant background highlight box:
   - Example: `Comment cette [nouvelle puce] a permis d'économiser plus de 14.000 €`
3. **Character Boundary Limits:**
   - Image headlines: Keep under **45 characters** for banners; **75 characters** for editorial cards.
   - Ad unit bold headlines: Keep under **40 characters** to prevent mobile truncation.
